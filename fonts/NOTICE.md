included in `licenses/` and copied into the website's `/fonts/licenses/`.

- Space Grotesk: The Space Grotesk Project Authors; [upstream notice](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk/blob/master/OFL.txt), `licenses/Space-Grotesk-OFL.txt`.
- JuliaMono: The JuliaMono Project Authors; [upstream notice](https://github.com/cormullion/juliamono/blob/master/LICENSE), `licenses/JuliaMono-OFL.txt`. The actual fonts come from the pinned nixpkgs `julia-mono` package.

IBM Plex and Space Grotesk WOFF2 subsets were inherited from the original
project checkout, via aanalyzer-classic. JuliaMono TTFs are added by the Nix
website build. No font files are relicensed.
